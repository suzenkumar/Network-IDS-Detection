How SentinelIDS decides

1

Normalise the flows

30 canonical features (27 numeric + protocol / service / flag) are extracted from your CSV, whatever the original column names were.

2

Two models vote

A class-balanced logistic-regression classifier predicts the attack category; an IsolationForest scores how unusual each flow is — catching attack types the classifier never saw.

3

Rules add the "why"

20 behavioural signatures — SYN floods, sweeps, brute-force logins, privilege escalation, exfiltration — turn a probability into a readable reason.

4

One risk score

risk = 0.50 × ML attack score
     + 0.25 × anomaly score
     + 0.25 × rule score
